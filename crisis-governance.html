Crisis Governance

Bitcoin Protocol Crisis Management Analysis

Governance Framework

Analysis of governance processes, actors, and decision-making mechanisms employed in Bitcoin protocol crisis resolution. This section examines the polycentric governance structures revealed through vulnerability management.

Crisis Actors

Analysis of key stakeholders involved in crisis resolution: core developers, miners, node operators, and community members.

Decision Mechanisms

Examination of formal and informal processes governing vulnerability disclosure, patch development, and deployment coordination.

Communication Channels

Study of information flows and coordination mechanisms during crisis situations, including mailing lists, IRC, and emergency alerts.

Consensus Building

Investigation of how consensus emerges around critical protocol changes and emergency measures during vulnerability responses.
